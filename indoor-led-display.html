What is an Indoor LED Display?

An indoor LED display is a digital screen composed of light-emitting diodes (LEDs) packed densely together to form high-definition images, video, and text. Unlike traditional LCD monitors or projectors, indoor LED panels offer true seamless integration, meaning multiple panels can be connected to create massive LED video walls without any visible bezels.

Because indoor audiences are closer to the screen, these displays utilize fine pitch LED display technology. This ensures sharp, crisp visuals without visible individual pixels. High refresh rates of 3840Hz ensure smooth video playback without flickering, crucial for broadcast and camera-facing applications.

Pixel Pitch Guide for Indoor LED

Choosing the correct pixel pitch is critical for your indoor LED screen. Pixel pitch refers to the distance in millimeters from the center of one LED pixel to the center of the adjacent pixel. A smaller number means higher resolution and closer optimal viewing distance.

  • P1.2 - P1.8 (Fine Pitch): Ideal for control rooms, broadcast studios, and boardrooms where viewers are 1 to 2 meters away.
  • P2.0 - P2.5: The industry standard for corporate lobbies, high-end retail, and conference rooms. Optimal viewing distance of 2 to 3 meters.
  • P3.0 - P4.0: Perfect for auditoriums, large event spaces, and churches. Optimal viewing distance of 3 to 5 meters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best pixel pitch for an indoor LED display?

For indoor LED displays, a pixel pitch between P1.2 and P3 is typically best, depending on viewing distance. P2.5 is standard for most corporate and retail environments, while P1.2 to P1.8 are ideal for control rooms and broadcast studios where viewers are very close to the screen.

How long do indoor LED video walls last?

High-quality indoor LED video walls, like those manufactured by Zorko Technologies, have a typical lifespan of 100,000 hours, which equates to over 11 years of 24/7 continuous operation.

Are indoor LED panels bright enough for well-lit rooms?

Yes. Indoor LED panels typically offer brightness levels ranging from 800 to 2000 nits, which is significantly brighter than standard LCD monitors or projectors, ensuring vibrant visuals even in brightly lit environments with natural sunlight.

What is the difference between indoor and outdoor LED displays?

Indoor LED displays feature finer pixel pitches for closer viewing distances and lower brightness (800-2000 nits) suitable for indoor lighting. Outdoor LED displays have larger pixel pitches, higher brightness (5000+ nits) to combat direct sunlight, and higher IP ratings (IP65+) for weather resistance.
