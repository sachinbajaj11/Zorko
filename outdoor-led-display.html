What Makes an Outdoor LED Display Different?

Deploying a screen outdoors presents unique challenges. An outdoor LED display must combat direct sunlight, torrential rain, dust, and extreme temperature fluctuations. Unlike indoor LED screens, outdoor LED billboards require specific engineering:

  • Ultra-High Brightness: While indoor screens peak at 1000 nits, an outdoor LED screen needs at least 5000 nits to compete with the sun. Our displays offer 5500 to 10000 nits for brilliant daytime visibility.
  • Weatherproofing (IP Rating): The harsh Indian monsoon demands uncompromising protection. Our cabinets feature IP65, IP66, or IP67 ratings, sealing out moisture and particulate matter.
  • Rugged Cabinet Construction: Outdoor installations face high winds and physical stress. We utilize robust die-cast aluminum or heavy-duty iron/steel cabinets for structural integrity.
  • Optimized Viewing Distance: Outdoor screens are typically viewed from afar. Choosing the right pixel pitch (from P3 for closer viewing to P10 for highway signs) is critical for visual clarity and budget optimization.

IP Rating & Weatherproofing Explained

When investing in an outdoor LED screen, understanding the Ingress Protection (IP) rating is paramount to ensure longevity and reliability.

The IP rating consists of two numbers: the first indicates protection against solids (dust), and the second indicates protection against liquids (water).

  • IP65: Dust-tight and protected against water jets from any angle. The standard for most reliable outdoor LED displays.
  • IP66: Dust-tight and protected against powerful water jets. Ideal for coastal areas or regions with heavy monsoons.
  • IP67: Dust-tight and protected against temporary immersion in water. Used for specialized, extreme-environment installations.

All Zorko outdoor LED displays are rigorously tested to meet IP65/IP66 standards, featuring specialized waterproof modules, sealed power supplies, and conformal-coated PCBs.

Outdoor LED Display Price Factors

The outdoor LED screen price in India varies based on several key technical specifications. Understanding these factors helps you optimize your investment:

  1. Pixel Pitch: A smaller pixel pitch (e.g., P3) means higher LED density and better resolution, which increases the cost compared to a larger pitch (e.g., P10).
  2. Screen Size: Total square meterage directly dictates the hardware cost.
  3. Cabinet Material: Standard iron cabinets are more economical, while lightweight die-cast aluminum cabinets command a premium for easier installation and better heat dissipation.
  4. Brightness Requirements: Standard 5500 nits displays are standard; ultra-high brightness (8000-10000 nits) modules for intense direct sunlight use specialized LEDs and cost more.
  5. Components: The quality of the LED lamps (e.g., Nationstar), driving ICs (e.g., Macroblock), and power supplies significantly impacts both price and longevity.
