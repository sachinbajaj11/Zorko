What is an LED Video Wall?

An LED video wall is a massive, highly engaging digital canvas created by connecting multiple, smaller LED modules or cabinets together. Unlike traditional LCD setups that rely on individual television screens arranged in a grid, an LED video wall display features a completely seamless surface. There are no black lines or bezels interrupting the picture.

The core technology relies on Surface-Mounted Device (SMD) LEDs or Chip-on-Board (COB) technology, where tiny diodes emit light and color directly. Because it is modular, a video wall can be scaled to virtually any size and configured into custom aspect ratios—curved, right-angled, or expansive ultra-wide canvases.

How to Choose the Right Video Wall Size & Pitch

Selecting the optimal LED video wall involves balancing the LED video wall price against your specific viewing requirements. The most important metric is Pixel Pitch (the distance in millimeters between each LED pixel).

A simple rule of thumb for viewing distance is: 1mm of pitch = 1 meter of viewing distance.

  • P1.2 to P1.5: Best for tight spaces, conference rooms, and control rooms where viewers are 1 to 2 meters away.
  • P2 to P3: Ideal for standard retail stores, corporate lobbies, and houses of worship with viewers 2 to 4 meters away.
  • P4 to P5: Cost-effective solutions for large indoor arenas or outdoor digital billboards where the audience is 5+ meters away.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an LED video wall?

An LED video wall is a large display surface consisting of multiple light-emitting diode (LED) panels tiled together seamlessly to form a single, cohesive screen. They offer high brightness, vivid colors, and no visible bezels.

How much does an LED video wall cost in India?

The price of an LED video wall in India varies based on pixel pitch, size, and application (indoor vs. outdoor). Fine-pitch indoor panels are generally more expensive per square meter than standard outdoor panels. As a direct manufacturer, Zorko offers competitive factory-direct pricing.

Is an LED video wall better than LCD?

Yes, LED video walls are typically superior to LCDs because they have zero bezels (truly seamless), higher brightness for daylight visibility, better contrast ratios, and a longer lifespan without color degradation.

What pixel pitch do I need for my video wall?

The pixel pitch depends on the viewing distance. For indoor control rooms and boardrooms (viewing from 1-3 meters), a pitch of P1.2 to P2.5 is ideal. For retail stores or lobbies (3-5 meters), P2.5 to P4 works well. For outdoor use, P5 or higher is standard.

Can a video wall display multiple sources?

Yes, with a video wall controller or processor, you can display multiple inputs simultaneously, create picture-in-picture layouts, or span a single ultra-high-definition video across the entire seamless video wall.
