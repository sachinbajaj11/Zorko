Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Zorko Technologies collects, how we use it, and your choices. It applies whenever you contact us or request a quotation.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you use our website — for example, when you fill out a contact form to request a quotation. This typically includes:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Any other details you include in your message

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used in the following ways:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To provide accurate quotations for our LED display manufacturing and installation services.
  • To communicate with you about your project, orders, and delivery.
  • To improve our website and customer service experience.

Data Protection

We are committed to protecting your personal data. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Cookies

Our website uses basic session cookies to enhance user experience and ensure the proper functioning of the site. These cookies do not track personal data across other websites.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to operate our website:

  • Cloudinary — for hosting and delivering images.
  • Google Fonts — for providing web typography.

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of data they may collect during your interaction with our site.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data that we hold. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Have a question?

If you have concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, we're happy to help.
