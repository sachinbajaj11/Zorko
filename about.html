About Zorko Technologies — India's Direct LED Display Manufacturer

Who We Are

Zorko Technologies stands at the forefront of visual innovation as India's premier direct manufacturer of LED displays and LED screens. In an industry often complicated by intermediaries, distributors, and unnecessary markups, we recognized the need for a streamlined, transparent approach. Our mission is simple: to design, engineer, and manufacture top-tier LED display solutions and deliver them directly to our clients across India, ensuring uncompromising quality at fair, factory-direct prices.

By producing our LED cabinets and assembling screens entirely in-house, we eliminate the middleman. This means our clients interact directly with the engineers and technicians who build their screens. Whether you are looking for a massive outdoor 3D billboard, a high-resolution indoor video wall for a corporate lobby, or durable rental cabinets for event production, Zorko Technologies is your direct source for brilliant, reliable visual technology.

Our Manufacturing Expertise

At the core of Zorko Technologies is our state-of-the-art manufacturing capability. We specialize in precision-engineered die-cast aluminum cabinets, which offer superior heat dissipation, structural integrity, and seamless alignment compared to standard sheet metal cabinets. Our meticulous assembly process ensures that every LED module, power supply, and receiving card is integrated flawlessly.

We manufacture screens across a comprehensive range of pixel pitches, from ultra-fine P0.5 for critical viewing environments like control rooms and broadcast studios, up to robust P10 for large-scale outdoor advertising. Every LED display undergoes rigorous, multi-stage quality control testing. We test for color uniformity, brightness consistency, weather resistance (for outdoor models), and sustained performance under stress, ensuring that when a Zorko display is installed, it performs flawlessly for years.

What We Manufacture

Our comprehensive product line covers every LED display requirement, serving diverse industries from retail and corporate to entertainment and advertising:

  • Indoor LED Displays: High-resolution screens with fine pixel pitches (P1.2–P4), perfect for boardrooms, retail stores, television studios, and auditoriums where close viewing distance demands pristine image quality. Read our pixel pitch guide to learn more.
  • Outdoor LED Displays: Ultra-bright (5500+ nits), weather-proof screens (IP65+ rated) designed to withstand the harsh Indian climate, ideal for digital billboards, sports stadiums, and outdoor advertising. See 3D billboard guide.
  • Transparent LED Screens: Innovative displays offering up to 95% transparency, allowing natural light into buildings while displaying stunning visuals on glass facades and storefronts.
  • Rental LED Cabinets: Lightweight, modular die-cast aluminum cabinets built for live events, concerts, and stage production.
  • Custom Video Walls: Tailor-made, seamless LED video wall configurations for corporate lobbies and control rooms, complete with 3840Hz refresh rate.
  • Mobile LED Displays: Truck, van, or delivery bag-mounted mobile LED advertising displays for fleet marketing. Check LED screen pricing in India.

Why Buy Direct?

Purchasing an LED display directly from the manufacturer offers substantial advantages over buying through dealers or distributors. Firstly, you can expect significant cost savings—often between 20% to 40%—by cutting out intermediary margins. Secondly, you gain a single point of accountability. From design and manufacturing to installation and warranty support, Zorko Technologies handles everything. If a technical issue arises, you speak directly with the experts who built your screen, ensuring faster, more accurate resolutions.

Buying direct also allows for unparalleled customization. Because we control the manufacturing process, we can tailor the size, shape, pixel pitch, and cabinet design to meet your exact specifications, rather than forcing a pre-packaged solution to fit your unique needs.
