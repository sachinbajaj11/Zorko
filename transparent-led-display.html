What is a Transparent LED Display?

A transparent LED display is an innovative visual solution that allows you to show high-resolution video content without blocking the view behind the screen. Unlike conventional digital signage that acts as a solid wall, a transparent LED screen integrates into its environment, offering a 'floating' visual effect.

These displays use specialized layouts, such as micro-LEDs embedded in thin films, mesh panels, or directly integrated into architectural glass. By leaving gaps between the pixels, natural sunlight can enter a building, and viewers inside can still see outside. The high brightness of the LEDs ensures the content remains vivid and eye-catching to people walking by, making it the ultimate tool for storefront window advertising and modern architectural integration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a transparent LED display work?

Transparent LED displays use micro-LEDs embedded in highly transparent materials like thin films or mesh structures. This allows light to pass through the gaps between pixels, creating a see-through effect while displaying bright images and videos.

Can transparent LED film be applied to existing windows?

Yes, transparent LED film is designed to be adhesive. It can be easily pasted onto existing glass windows, facades, or storefronts without altering the architectural structure.

Are transparent LED screens visible in direct sunlight?

Absolutely. High-end transparent LED displays and meshes (like our HoloMesh series) can achieve brightness levels exceeding 7000 nits, making them perfectly visible even under direct sunlight.

What is the maximum transparency I can get?

Depending on the pixel pitch and the type of product (film vs mesh), transparency can range from 70% up to 95%. Higher pixel pitches typically offer higher transparency as there is more space between the LEDs.
